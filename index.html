About

Fixing the strategy before performance drops

I focus on the "why" behind customer behavior—specifically how people react when things change, like price hikes or platform updates. My approach ensures that lifecycle systems aren't just hitting this week's targets, but are built to adapt and protect revenue for the long haul.

Strategic Advantage Policy training taught me to look at the external incentives and constraints that shape how people act. I see the shifts in the market before they ever show up in your performance data.

Building for what happens next

Success requires more than just reacting to data. By layering market intelligence—like industry cycles and economic shifts—onto customer behavior, I build systems that reach people exactly when they are most ready to engage. This approach works for any brand facing a changing market.
